Australian cultural participation, 2010–2025

Back to the Show?

In 2025 a record 74% of Australians went to a live arts event or festival, but has that comeback reached every age group, region and budget equally?

By Adam Rizq Rozman · FIT3179 Data Visualisation 2 · October 2026

01 A record year

In 2025, 74% of Australians aged 15 and over (15.4 million people) attended a live arts event or festival, the highest level since the survey began in 2009; each square in the chart represents one in every 100 Australians.

02 Fifteen years of live performance

Ticket sales fell from 23.9 million in 2019 to 7.8 million in 2020 before climbing to a record 31.4 million in 2024, while revenue grew even faster as the average ticket price rose from $86 in 2010 to $115.

03 The COVID shock: who came back?

Between 2017–18 and 2021–22, a period that included COVID-19 lockdowns, attendance at cultural venues and events fell in every age group, most sharply among 45–54-year-olds (down 21.6 percentage points).

04 Where you live matters

Attendance fell in every state and territory between 2017–18 and 2021–22, with Victoria dropping furthest (from 84% to 61%); hover over a state to highlight it in the bar chart.

05 The price of culture

Cost is now the biggest barrier to attending the arts, named by 60% of Australians in 2025 compared with 55% in 2022.
